## Flash with Balena Etcher

Download Etcher from the [official Balena Etcher site](https://etcher.balena.io/). It writes a complete bootable image and verifies what it wrote.

> [!DANGER] Etcher erases the USB target
> Unplug other removable drives first. Match the USB’s capacity and model before clicking Flash.

1. Download and install Etcher for Windows.
2. Insert an 8 GB or larger USB stick.
3. Open Etcher and click **Flash from file**.
4. Select the ZimaOS image you downloaded.
5. Click **Select target**.
6. Choose the USB—not the old PC’s internal drive or your backup drive.
7. Click **Flash** and approve administrator access if Windows asks.
8. Wait for both writing and verification to finish.
9. Safely eject the USB.

## If Windows asks to format it

Choose **Cancel**. Windows may not understand the Linux partitions Etcher created. That is expected and does not mean the flash failed.

## Etcher troubleshooting

**USB does not appear:** reconnect it, use another port, close other disk tools, and check Disk Management. **Flash fails:** download the image first if you used a URL, retry, then try another port or USB. **Verification fails:** do not install from it; reflash, redownload the image, or replace the USB. Balena says failed flashes are commonly a faulty drive, adapter, card, or USB port.
